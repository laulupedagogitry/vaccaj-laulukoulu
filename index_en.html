Practical vocal method - Metodo pratico by Nicolo Vaccaj

This site is produced by the Sibelius Academy Department of Vocal Music and the Sibelius Academy Innovation Centre. It includes notations, lyrics with instructions for pronunciation, and the English translations of the songs in Nicolo Vaccaj's Metodo pratico collection , with sample recordings and background information. The material is intended for all students and teachers of classical singing as well as pianists working with singers, and others interested.

The site was published on 6 March 2007.

The English version was published on 26 June 2009.

Enjoy the singing lessons!
