Resistance makes love flare up:

[kon ki 'tʃede o ki s ar'rende]
con chi cede, o chi s'arrende
to him that succumbs or surrenders,

['mai si 'barbaro non ɛ]

it is never cruel.

When he encounters resistance, Cupid *
flares up, but he is never cruel
to those that surrender.

* (Cupid imprisoned / By Metastasio)
