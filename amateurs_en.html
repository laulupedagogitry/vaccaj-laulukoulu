Amateurs

In his preface to Metodo pratico, Vaccaj names amateurs as his target group. This group of students wanted to sing less demanding songs, not arias or oratorios. Thus, Vaccaj's method is more a chamber singing than an opera singing school. This is why Metodo pratico is not considered a difficult method, and it has gained great popularity.
